# Mercedes F1 EDA — Findings Summary

Historical, descriptive findings from the supplied 2021–2025 Mercedes-only workbook.

- **Highest team weekend-points total:** 2021 with 613.5 points, including the source's sprint points.
- **Lowest team weekend-points total:** 2023 with 409.0 points. The number of GP and sprint weekends differs by season, so compare this with points per GP weekend and the split between race and sprint points.
- **Most wins:** 9 in 2021.
- **Most podium finishes:** 28 in 2021.
- **Grid–finish association:** Spearman rho = 0.548 (n = 208 classified results). This measures association and does not prove causation.
- **Highest recorded DNF rate:** 2021 at 11.36% of driver entries; DQs are reported separately.
- **Qualifying coverage:** usable `Quali Pos` is available for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025; normal qualifying fields are absent for all 2021 records.

## Scope limitations

- Standard qualifying data is unavailable for 2021.
- The workbook contains no pit-stop timing, tyre stint/compound, telemetry, safety-car, weather, or retirement-cause tables.
- This is a Mercedes-only dataset, so it cannot independently establish competitive rank versus other constructors.
- Association and descriptive patterns should not be interpreted as proof of causation.
